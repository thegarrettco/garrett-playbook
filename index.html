The Garrett Family Foundation

The Garrett Family Foundation is dedicated to relieving burdens of individuals and supporting organizations dedicated to the same cause. Our mission is to foster resilience, ignite hope, and carve pathways to success for those facing obstacles.

Giving Back

Investing in the communities we call home

The Garrett Family Foundation channels our success into the people and places around us — supporting local causes, families in need, and organizations doing meaningful work. Every project we complete is a chance to strengthen a community, and the Foundation is how we make that commitment lasting. When you get involved, you're extending the Garrett mission beyond our walls.

How to Get Involved with Garrett Family Foundation

1
Employee Match

Every donation you make to the Garrett Family Foundation goes twice as far. All employee gifts are eligible for a dollar-for-dollar match, up to a specified amount per fiscal year, as outlined in our giving policy. It's a simple way to double your impact — your generosity, matched by the company, reaching even more people who need it.

Community Rewards

You can support the Garrett Family Foundation every time you shop at Kroger, King Soopers, or City Market. Pair your account with GFF and help us help others while you grocery shop. This will cost you nothing and the foundation will earn a percentage of everything you spend! Here, every transaction makes a positive difference!
